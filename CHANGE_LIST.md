# Construction Mod: Changes & Additions

This document lists all the changes and additions the Construction Mod makes to the base game. It serves both as a changelog, so players can see what’s been added, or as a guide, meant to help you find opportunities to improve your efficiency in Construction. This list does **NOT** include changes that furniture upgrades cause to items or mechanics, unless further clarification is required.

Entries marked with `[TotH]` or `[AoD]` indicate Throne of the Herald or Atlas of Discovery changes.

---

## Misc
- Added a new Autumn-themed background in the settings.

---

## Gamemodes
- `[AoD]` Added Relics, a lesser Relic, and a Master Relic to Construction for Ancient Relics-enabled gamemodes.

---

## Shop

### New Purchases
- Added the **"Saw"** items in the Skill Upgrades section. They work like the Axe, Fishing Rod, or Pickaxe tool chains, but for Construction. They also increase Efficiency effects and decrease intervals.

### Changed Purchases
- Axe and Pickaxe now also cost Planks and Nails along with their gold costs. Fishing Rod now costs Planks and Straps. This encourages players to interact with the Construction skill or obtain materials from enemy loot tables.  
- The **"Perpetual Haste"** purchase (unlocked upon completing the Air God Dungeon) now also gives `-15%` interval to Construction. This is reflected in its description and picture.

---

## Combat

### New Enemies
- **Barn Raiser** (Farmlands): Drops Normal Planks, Iron Nails, Clay Bricks, and Green D-hide Straps. Rarely drops Oak Planks, Steel Nails, or Limestone Bricks.  
- **Iron Giant** (Giant's Dungeon): Good source of Nails.  
- **Basic Arcane Golem**, **Advanced Arcane Golem**, **Expert Arcane Golem** (Wizard Tower): Drop Rune Essence and various Runes. Drop Clay, Limestone, and Marble Bricks respectively. Expert Arcane Golem also has a chance to drop a special item.

### Changed Enemies
*(Note: new items added without decreasing rare item chances)*

- Goblins can now drop Planks; Ranged Goblins can drop Nails.  
- Confused Pirate (Sandy Shores) drops remains of the ship he floated in on — good source of Planks.  
- Green, Blue, Red, and Black Dragons have a `35%` chance to drop [Leather Slivers](#fletching) instead of Leather. Amount dropped ≈ 3–4× regular Leather.  
- `[AoD]` Dark Quarry's golems can drop Bricks corresponding to their material.

### Changed Chests
*(Note: new items added without decreasing rare item chances)*

- **Elder Chest** can now contain [Elder Dragonhide Slivers](#fletching) instead of normal Elder Dragonhide.

## Township
- Added new trades at The Trader. You can now trade for **Beam Boxes** if your Fletching, Woodcutting, and Township levels are sufficiently high.  
  - Basic Box → [Wood Beams](#crafting): Normal, Oak, Teak  
  - Regular Box → Teak, Mahogany, Redwood

## Fishing

<details>
<summary>The Lake's third Tier upgrade additions</summary>

- Adds 3 new items to the Fishing Special pool:  
  - **The Tar Jar** (`175/6722 ≈ 2.6%`) gives bonuses to Thieving and Agility, `600` per catch.  
  - **The Cursed Hook** (`23/6722 ≈ 0.33%`) greatly increases chance to find treasure but costs money per catch. Cost is inversely correlated with the target's sale value.  
  - **The Mariner's Tie** (`2/6722 ≈ 0.03%`) can be used with the Sailor's Top to upgrade it into the Mariner's Blouse, giving both Fish and Special items on a Special roll.

*Note: The Fishing pool's Special Table was changed so rare items’ chances were not decreased.*

## Fletching
- Added **"Wood Beams"** category: Normal, Oak, Teak, Mahogany, Redwood.  
- Beams are used to make Planks, replacing regular logs when crafting Arrow Shafts. Making arrow shafts from Willow, Maple, Yew, and Magic logs has been removed to balance Tree Types.  
- Added a bonus of `+1` additional Wood Beam quantity made to Fletching 50% Mastery Pool Bonus.

---

## Crafting
- Added **"Leather Slivers"** category: Green, Red, Blue, Black, Ancient Dragonhide.  
- Slivers are used to make Straps, replacing normal Dragonhide when upgrading Dragonhide Armor. Higher-tier Dragonhide outputs fewer Slivers. Encourages combat/crafting rather than buying upgrades.

---

## Herblore
- Added **"Twice-Measured"** skill potion at level 70 Herblore. Provides boost in Efficiency chance when making construction materials *or* Efficiency potency when making furniture.

---

## Summoning
- Added **Bee** Tier 3 Summoning Familiar (80 Summoning), marks unlocked while training Construction. Crafted using [Wood Beams](#crafting) or Planks. Provides `+0.25` extra material/progress when activating Efficiency. Synergies with Monkey, Pig, Salamander, Bear, and Devil.  
- `[TotH]` Bee also gains synergies with Owl and Beaver.  
- Nerfed Bear and Devil synergy: now gives `+15%` Bonfire Interval (down from 100%) and now also spends on lighting a bonfire and firemaking action.

---

## Astrology
- `[TotH]` Added Construction Skill and buffs to the Hamriel star (the one that gave Township bonuses)

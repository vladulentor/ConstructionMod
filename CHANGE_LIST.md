This document lists all the changes and additions the Construction Mod makes to the base game. It serves both as a changelog—so players can see what’s been added or as a guide, meant to help you find every opportunity to improve your efficiency in Construction. 

Entries marked with [TotH] indicate Throne of the Herald changes.

Combat:
-Added "Barn Raiser" enemy to Farmlands. Drops early-game planks and nails, offering a combat-way of building up construction.
-Added "Construct Giant" enemy to Giant's Dungeon. You can get many nails from fighting him, among other things.

Herblore:
-Added "Twice-Measured" skill potion at level 35. This will give you a boost in multiple craft chance when making construction materials *or* potency when making furniture.

Astrology:
-[TotH] Added Construction Skill and many buffs to the Hamriel star (the one that gave Township bonuses)
